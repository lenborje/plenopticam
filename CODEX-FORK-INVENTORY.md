## Gentoo downstream

The advertised package is in the **third-party autogenerated `pypi` overlay**, not evidence of official Gentoo testing. [Package listing](https://gpo.zugaina.org/dev-python/plenopticam), [ebuild](https://data.gpo.zugaina.org/pypi/dev-python/plenopticam/plenopticam-0.9.3.ebuild), [directory/Manifest](https://data.gpo.zugaina.org/pypi/dev-python/plenopticam/).

The 1,336-byte ebuild declares `PYTHON_COMPAT=( python{3_11,3_12,3_13,3_14} )`, `DISTUTILS_USE_PEP517=standalone`, inherits `python-r1 gs-pypi`, and explicitly sets `RESTRICT="test"`. It installs the unchanged PyPI 0.9.3 sdist. Its Manifest contains only the sdist, ebuild and metadata.xml: **no patch files**, no `PATCHES`, no package-specific prepare hook. It retains tifffile `~2022.4.8` and SciPy >=1.6; it drops exact constraints for color-matcher/PyInstaller at the package-manager level, but does not demonstrate runtime fixes. Changelog's target regeneration is attributed to Regeneration Bot, `69e1ce8751e36cee053008fc6bc07a396f3dd063` (2025-08-14). Its license field says `GNU GPL V3.0` for PlenoptiCam; no ebuild code is being copied, and the generator/overlay's own license has not been independently established. There are no useful downstream patches to adopt.
